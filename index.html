Requirements

Entry Requirements

TECOHAS admits candidates who meet the minimum educational requirements for their chosen program. All programs require a genuine passion for healthcare.

  • O'Level Results: Minimum of 5 credit passes in WAEC or NECO (including English Language and relevant science subjects) — maximum of 2 sittings accepted
  • JAMB / UTME: Admission is open with or without JAMB — JAMB score is not compulsory
  • Birth Certificate: Valid original birth certificate or sworn declaration of age
  • Citizenship: Nigerian citizenship — evidence via National ID, NIN slip, or International Passport
  • Medical Fitness: Certificate of medical fitness from a registered medical practitioner

Our History

Training Healthcare Heroes Since Day One

TEMTAB College of Health and Allied Science (TECOHAS), also known as TECOHTECH and TECOHEALTH, is a government-recognized health sciences institution located at Opposite NNPC, Ilorin/Old-Jebba Highway, Oke-Oyi, Kwara State, Nigeria.

The college was established to bridge the gap in skilled healthcare manpower in Kwara State and across Nigeria. TECOHAS runs 11 accredited programs spanning community health, allied health sciences, and emergency medicine, producing graduates who serve in hospitals, clinics, NGOs, and government health agencies across Nigeria.

🎯 Our Mission

To provide quality, affordable, and accessible health science education that equips graduates with practical skills for Nigeria's healthcare sector.

🌟 Our Vision

To become the foremost college of health and allied sciences in North Central Nigeria, producing competent and compassionate healthcare professionals.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply for admission to TECOHAS?▼
Visit our Admissions page, complete the online application form, pay the ₦5,000 application form fee via Flutterwave, and upload the required documents. You will receive a confirmation email with your Application Reference Number.
What are the minimum O'Level requirements?▼
You need a minimum of 5 credits at O'Level (WAEC/NECO/NABTEB) including English Language, Biology, and Chemistry or Physics. Two sittings are acceptable.
How do I access the student portal?▼
Click "Portal Login" in the top navigation bar, select "Student" tab, and enter your Matriculation Number and password. If you have not yet set up your account, visit the ICT unit with your admission letter.
How do I pay school fees?▼
Log into the student portal and navigate to "Fee Payment." You can pay via Flutterwave using a debit card or bank transfer. Always save your payment receipt.
Are TECOHAS programs nationally accredited?▼
Yes. All TECOHAS programs are accredited by the relevant professional regulatory bodies in Nigeria, including HEFAMAA, NBTE, MLSCN, PCN, and EHORECON.
